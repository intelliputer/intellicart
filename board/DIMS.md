## 44-pin edge connector 

The geometry of the edge connector is contained in the following.

| Feature | Dimension | Status / Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Total electrical contacts | 44 | 22 contacts on each side of the cartridge PCB |
| Contacts per PCB side | 22 | One row of edge fingers per PCB surface |
| Contact pitch | 2.54 mm (0.100 in) | Cartridge contact spacing |
| Number of pitch intervals | 21 | 22 contacts create 21 intervals |
| First-to-last terminal center span | 53.34 mm (2.100 in) | 21 × 2.54 mm; agrees with the measured ~53.4 mm |
| Measured terminal-to-terminal span | ~53.4 mm | User measurement; presumably center-to-center |
| Overall contact-array width | Approximately 58 mm | Depends on terminal width and end margins |
| Cartridge PCB thickness | Approximately 1.6 mm (0.062 in) | Verify against the original cartridge PCB |
| Replacement connector solder-tail pitch | May be 3.96 mm (0.156 in) | Connector-specific; not the cartridge contact pitch |
